# Velocity & Estimation Reasoning 

## Total Story points: 52

## Estimated team Velocity: 15 Story points per Sprint
The above estimated velocity is based on a 4-5 member scrum team, working in 2-weeks sprints. This also allows for testing, bug fixes and reviews.

## Estimated number of Sprints

Total Story Points / Team velocity = 52/15 = 3.5
Estimated sprint required : 4 sprints

## Reasoning
I did some research and usually it seems that the teams median historical velocity is taken to determine the teams capacity. I went on an estimate of a 4-5 member team from the resources I found online. I estimated 4 sprints, to rather have more time than less. I would approach this by completing the core functionality of the booking system in the first sprints, prioritising them, as they are essential for the system. The other features will be added in a later sprint.
